How to check Pakistan's latest petrol price

Find the latest notified rate, confirm when it takes effect, and avoid stale price lists.

Read the guide↗

Petrol rates in Pakistan can be revised through official government notifications. We do not display a live price here, so this guide will not become a source of outdated figures.

When a change is announced, look for the official Government of Pakistan notification. Check the product name (for example, motor spirit/petrol), the rate, and the date and time it becomes effective. Do not assume a headline or an undated social-media image is current.

If you cannot find the primary notice, compare recent reports from reputable news organizations that cite it, then confirm with your fuel station. Rates and implementation details can change; always verify before you travel.

02SMALL HABITS, BETTER MILEAGE
FUEL-SAVING TIPS4 MIN READ

Get more from every litre

Small driving and maintenance habits that can help you use fuel more efficiently.

Read the guide↗

Smooth driving is a good place to start. Accelerate gently, keep your speed steady where conditions allow, and look ahead so you can slow down gradually instead of braking sharply.

Check tyre pressure regularly against your vehicle maker's recommendation, keep up with routine maintenance, and remove heavy items you do not need to carry. Under-inflated tyres and neglected maintenance can affect efficiency and safety.

Fuel savings vary with your vehicle, route, traffic, and weather. Compare fuel use over several similar trips before deciding whether a change helped.

03✳CAR CARE / STAY SAFE
CAR CARE & SAFETY4 MIN READ

Fuel up safely, care for your car

A practical pump-side checklist, plus one essential habit before every refill.

Read the guide↗

Before refuelling, check your vehicle manual or fuel-door label for the recommended petrol type and octane rating. If you are unsure, ask your vehicle maker or an authorized service centre rather than guessing.

At the station, follow signs and staff instructions. Turn off the engine, keep flames and smoking away, and never leave the nozzle unattended. Stop when the pump clicks off; do not try to top up further.

Keep up with routine servicing and tyre-pressure checks to help care for your car. If fuel spills, alert station staff and follow their instructions.

Frequently asked questions

Petrol prices, fuel use, and car care.

How do I check the latest official petrol price in Pakistan?+

Check the latest Government of Pakistan notification. Confirm that it names petrol (motor spirit), shows the rate, and states its effective date and time. Treat undated posts and older price lists as unverified.

What if there is no verified new notification?+

Do not guess or rely on a circulating screenshot. We leave unverified rates unpublished. Check official Government channels again and confirm the displayed rate with your fuel station before refuelling.

How can I use less fuel on everyday drives?+

Accelerate gently, avoid unnecessary idling, plan trips where practical, and keep tyres at the pressure recommended in your owner's manual. Regular servicing helps too. Results depend on your vehicle, route, traffic, and weather.

Which petrol is safe and right for my car?+

Use the fuel type and minimum octane rating specified in your owner's manual or on the fuel-door label. If the recommendation is unclear, ask the vehicle maker or an authorized service centre. Do not switch fuel types based on unverified claims.

Does the country selector cover every country or update prices in real time?+

No. A current free source we verified does not provide a legally reusable, no-cost live API for every country. GlobalPetrolPrices advertises a subscription API for 135 countries; its public list has fewer locations. Its public pages show local-currency gasoline references, usually updated weekly and monthly in some regulated markets—not real-time rates or every local grade. We link to those pages instead of copying prices. Check each page's source update date and confirm the legal effective rate with the local government or regulator.
